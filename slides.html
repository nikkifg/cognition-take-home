Build vs. buy

Replacing Power Apps: An Internal Platform Built with Devin

The pitch

Build one governed internal platform with Devin. Every internal app sits on top.

Power Apps' real value is the foundation underneath: Microsoft sign-in, roles, approval flows, audit trails, compliance.

The question isn't whether we can build apps. It's whether we can replace that foundation.

Why build

Three reasons

01

Tools work our way

Tools work exactly the way the team wants, with no customization ceiling. Faster than model-driven Power Apps, which are generated from metadata at runtime.

02

Shipping is fast

Once the foundation exists, Devin does the building.

03

Cost

Power Apps charges per user, so the bill grows with hiring. An owned platform costs the same at 50 or 500 users.

Demo 1 · Approvals

Nobody approves their own refund

  1. Sign in as Finance Admin 1 and request a refund.
  2. Try to approve it: blocked, you can't approve your own request.
  3. Switch to Finance Admin 2 and approve.
  4. The refund goes straight to the payment processor.

The separation-of-duties rule lives in the foundation, so every future tool gets it automatically.

Demo 2 · Sensitive data

SSNs are hidden by default

  1. Open a KYC case: the SSN is masked.
  2. As a senior compliance reviewer, reveal it.
  3. Open /admin/audit: it records who looked and when, without storing the number.
  4. Sign in as an analyst: no reveal at all.
Demo 3 · Access

Admins can see, not approve

  1. Sign in as admin and open the KYC queue.
  2. The queue is visible, but approving customers isn't allowed.

Responsibilities stay separate, even for the most privileged role.

Demo 4 · A new tool

A payouts tool from a few sentences

  1. The brief: a few sentences asking for a tool to approve payout bank-account changes.
  2. The PR: Devin built it following the platform's rules.
  3. The running app: approvals, hidden account numbers and audit logging already work.

What it can't replace

Two honest gaps

01

The ops team building tools themselves

Now they describe what they need, Devin builds, an engineer approves. A real change in who does the work.

02

Microsoft running everything

Hosting, security updates and uptime become our responsibility.

Risks

What to plan for

Greater maintenance

AI makes building software cheaper, not owning it. Most of a tool's cost is updates, bug fixes and security patches, so budget for it from day one.

Compliance

Move regulated workflows last, once the foundation proves secure.
